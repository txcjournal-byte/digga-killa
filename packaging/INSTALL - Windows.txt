TrapWin Sampler 1.0.0 - Windows installation (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)
====================================================================

1. Close your DAW.
2. Run "TrapWinSampler-1.0.0-Windows-Setup.exe" and follow the steps.
   The VST3 plugin goes to    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\TrapWin Sampler.vst3
   The standalone app goes to C:\Program Files\TrapWin\TrapWin Sampler
3. Start the DAW and rescan plugins
   (FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins).
4. Load "TrapWin Sampler" as an instrument / generator.

How it works
------------
Drop a song or loop (WAV, MP3, AIFF, FLAC) on the vinyl. TrapWin Sampler finds
where its sections start (kick comes in, melody starts, drop, break) and cuts
them into 8 and 16 bar loops, plus clean one-shots (kick, 808, snare, hats,
chords, notes). Click a sample to edit it below, then drag it straight into
your DAW as WAV or MIDI.

If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC" for the installer,
click "More info" > "Run anyway".
