TrapWin Sample  -  https://trapwin.com
======================================

Drop a song or loop on the vinyl: TrapWin Sample finds where its sections
start (kick comes in, melody starts, drop, break) and cuts them into 8 and
16 bar loops, plus clean one-shots (kick, 808, snare, hats, chords, notes).
Edit any sample below, then drag it straight into your DAW as WAV or MIDI.


WINDOWS (FL Studio, Ableton Live, Cubase, Studio One, Reaper ...)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy the folder "TrapWin Sample.vst3" from Windows\ to
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
3. Start the DAW and rescan plugins
   (FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins).
4. Load "TrapWin Sample" as an instrument / generator.

"TrapWin Sample.exe" runs on its own, without a DAW.


MAC (Logic Pro, Ableton Live, FL Studio, GarageBand ...)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Unzip "TrapWin Sample Mac.zip".
2. Copy  "TrapWin Sample.component"  to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   Copy  "TrapWin Sample.vst3"       to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   (Library is hidden: in Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and paste the path.)
3. "TrapWin Sample.app" is the standalone version: drag it into Applications.
4. Restart the DAW. Logic: the plugin is under AU Instruments > TrapWin.

If macOS says the plugin "can't be checked for malicious software", this
build is not yet notarized: right-click the app > Open, or allow it in
System Settings > Privacy & Security.
