TrapWin Sampler 1.0.0 - macOS installation (Apple Silicon & Intel, macOS 11+)
============================================================================

1. Unzip the download (double-click it).
2. In Finder press Shift+Cmd+G and go to:  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/
   - copy "TrapWin Sampler.vst3"      into  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   - copy "TrapWin Sampler.component" into  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/   (AU - Logic Pro, GarageBand)
   (You can also use ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/... for your user only.)
3. Optional: copy "TrapWin Sampler.app" (standalone) into /Applications.
4. Open your DAW and rescan plugins.
   Logic Pro: AU Instruments > TrapWin > TrapWin Sampler.

How it works
------------
Drop a song or loop (WAV, MP3, AIFF, FLAC) on the vinyl. TrapWin Sampler finds
where its sections start (kick comes in, melody starts, drop, break) and cuts
them into 8 and 16 bar loops, plus clean one-shots (kick, 808, snare, hats,
chords, notes). Click a sample to edit it below, then drag it straight into
your DAW as WAV or MIDI.
